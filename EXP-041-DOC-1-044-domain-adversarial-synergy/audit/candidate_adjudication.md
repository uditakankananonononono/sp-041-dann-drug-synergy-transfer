Reasons: it has a versioned open primary archive, an explicit locked external study, drug-pair-grouped leakage control, architecture-matched ERM comparator, paired clustered uncertainty, quantitative gates, and a valid useful-negative interpretation if transfer fails. Its primary claim is narrower and more testable than the alternatives.

- DOC-2-043: only 13 locked-test patients, the source paper already links spatial architecture to survival, and the development-permutation/test-improvement design has weak inferential resolution. It remains a possible redesigned future experiment.
